### Option D: Dark Mode Glassmorphic (Neon & Obsidian)

- **Design Elements**: Frost-blurred glass cards, glowing neon accents (emerald greens, teals, and ambers), golden-white typography, and a rich, deep obsidian background.
- **Layout**: Sleek weather tracker card at the top, a glowing action shelf, and a card-based social announcements feed.
- **Vibe**: Futuristic, premium, and extremely modern.

### Option E: Warm Eco-Theme (Friendly & Organic)

- **Design Elements**: Soft pastel forest greens, natural clay tones, warm yellows, and rounded corners with realistic soft shadows.
- **Layout**: Stylized modern village landscape illustration header, clean quick-action grids, and announcements.
- **Vibe**: Friendly, community-oriented, fresh, and organic.

### Option F: Sleek FinTech Minimalist (Vibrant & Bold)

- **Design Elements**: Bold sans-serif fonts, crisp borders, micro-shadows, and a high-contrast palette (deep violet, vibrant mint, and soft orange).
- **Layout**: Large bold greetings, modern vector-illustrated activity buttons in card stacks.
- **Vibe**: Trendy, fast, clean, and highly professional.
